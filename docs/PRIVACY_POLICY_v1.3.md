**Privacy Policy**

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Age Calculator mobile application
("the app") handles your information.

**Summary**

Everything the app does happens on your device. The app has no account, no
servers, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not have permission to
use the internet. No personal information is collected by us or shared with
anyone.

**Information stored on your device**

Events you create (such as birthdays, exams or trips), their reminders, and
your theme preference are stored only on your device, in the app's private
storage. They are not uploaded or synced anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or
using Settings → Delete all events, removes them.

**Contacts (optional)**

If you choose "Birthdays from contacts", the app asks for permission to read
your contacts. It then reads only the names and the birthday and anniversary
dates saved in your contacts, shows them to you, and saves only the ones you
select as events on your device. The app never changes your contacts, never
reads other contact details such as phone numbers or email addresses for any
other purpose, and never sends contact data off your device. You can revoke
this permission at any time in Android Settings.

**Calendar (optional)**

If you choose "Events from calendar", the app asks for permission to read
your calendars. It reads the titles and times of your events for the next
six months, shows them to you, and saves only the ones you select as events
on your device. The app never changes your calendars and never sends
calendar data off your device. You can revoke this permission at any time in
Android Settings.

**Notifications and alarms**

If you turn on reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications
and, optionally, to schedule exact alarms so reminders arrive on time. These
are used only to remind you about your own events.

**Age calculations**

All age calculations are performed entirely on your device.

**Advertising, analytics and third parties**

The app does not display advertisements, does not use analytics, and does
not include third-party services that collect personal information.

**Children's privacy**

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any
information from children.

**Changes to this Privacy Policy**

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be
published on this page with a new effective date.
