Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Age Calculator mobile application ("the app") handles your information.

Summary

Everything the app does happens on your device. The app has no account, no servers, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not have permission to use the internet. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Information Stored on Your Device

Events you create (such as birthdays, exams or trips), their reminders, and your theme preference are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are not uploaded or synced anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Delete all events, removes them.

Contacts (Optional)

If you choose Birthdays from contacts, the app asks for permission to read your contacts. It then reads only the names and the birthday and anniversary dates saved in your contacts, shows them to you, and saves only the ones you select as events on your device.

  • The app never changes, adds or deletes anything in your contacts.
  • The app does not use other contact details, such as phone numbers or email addresses.
  • Contact data never leaves your device.
  • You can revoke this permission at any time in Android Settings.

Calendar (Optional)

If you choose Events from calendar, the app asks for permission to read your calendars. It reads the titles and times of your events for the next six months, shows them to you, and saves only the ones you select as events on your device.

  • The app never changes, adds or deletes anything in your calendars.
  • Calendar data never leaves your device.
  • You can revoke this permission at any time in Android Settings.

Notifications and Alarms

If you use reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications and, optionally, to schedule exact alarms so reminders arrive on time. These are used only to remind you about your own events.

Age Calculations

All age calculations are performed entirely on your device. No data is sent to external servers.

Internet Connection

The app works fully offline and does not request permission to access the internet.

Permissions

The app only requests permissions that are required for its features, and only when you use them:

  • Contacts – to import birthdays and anniversaries, when you choose to.
  • Calendar – to import calendar events, when you choose to.
  • Notifications – to show reminders for your events.
  • Alarms – to deliver reminders on time and restore them after a restart.

The app does not access your location, photos, camera, microphone, or any other personal information.

Advertising

The current version of the app does not display advertisements.

Analytics

The app does not use analytics services to collect user behavior or usage information.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party services that collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any future changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.
